# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make the Lumière community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all. We are committed to fostering an environment that respects the dignity and contributions of everyone, regardless of background, identity, or status.

## Encouraged Behaviors

1. Respecting the **purpose of our community**.
2. Engaging **kindly and honestly** with others.
3. Respecting **different viewpoints**.
4. **Taking responsibility** for our actions.
5. Giving and accepting **constructive feedback**.

## Restricted Behaviors

The following are strictly prohibited:

* **Harassment** — Violating boundaries or unwanted personal attention.
* **Character attacks** — Insulting or demeaning comments.
* **Stereotyping** — Discrimination based on immutable traits.
* **Endangerment** — Threats of violence or harm.
* **Misleading identity** — Impersonating others or evading bans.
* **Disruption** — Deliberately derailing discussions, spamming issues/PRs, or interfering with others' ability to contribute.

## Reporting an Issue

If an incident occurs, please report it promptly.

**To report a violation:**

* [Open a ticket in the Lumière Labs Discord](https://discord.gg/RZP3A9dq7S), or
* Email **[conduct@lumierelabs.xyz](mailto:conduct@lumierelabs.xyz)** if you'd rather not use Discord.

Include as much detail as you can — what happened, when, and where — along with screenshots or message links if available.

Reports are handled by the Lumière Labs maintainer team. Reporter identity is kept strictly confidential throughout the process. We investigate available evidence — logs, messages, and witness accounts — and aim to resolve every report fairly and promptly.

## Enforcement Ladder

We use the following guidelines to determine the appropriate response to violations:

1. **Warning** — For minor or single incidents. A private warning and a request for an apology or correction.
2. **Temporarily Limited Activities** — For repeated minor violations. A cooldown period where access to certain channels or repo interactions is restricted.
3. **Temporary Suspension** — For serious violations or a pattern of behavior. A time-bound ban from the GitHub repository and Discord server.
4. **Permanent Ban** — For extreme cases (hate speech, persistent harassment, threats). Permanent removal from all Lumière Labs spaces.

Maintainers may skip earlier steps for severe first-time violations.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Lumière community spaces — this repository, its issues and pull requests, the Lumière Discord server — and to anyone officially representing the project in public.
