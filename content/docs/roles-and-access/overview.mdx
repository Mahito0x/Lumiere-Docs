Lumière's role tools cover four different situations: giving new members roles automatically, keeping roles across a rejoin, giving out roles that expire on their own, and managing roles for large groups of members at once.

## What's in this section

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Auto-Roles on Join](/roles-and-access/auto-roles-on-join) | Automatically give roles to members when they join |
| [Persistent Roles](/roles-and-access/persistent-roles) | Roles that survive a member leaving and rejoining |
| [Temporary Roles](/roles-and-access/temporary-roles) | Roles that expire automatically after a set time |
| [Bulk Role Management](/roles-and-access/bulk-role-management) | Assign or remove a role for many members at once |
| [Join-Date Roles](/roles-and-access/join-date-roles) | Assign roles based on when members joined |

## Before you start

Every command here requires **Manage Roles**, and Lumière can only assign or remove roles that sit **below** its own role in your server's role hierarchy. If a command fails unexpectedly, that's the first thing to check — see [Adding the Bot](/getting-started/adding-the-bot#role-position-matters).
