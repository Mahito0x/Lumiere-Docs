---
title: Your Stats
description: View your own activity, control your privacy, and get weekly reports.
---

## View your stats

```
/stats user member period
```

Leave `member` empty to view your own stats. `period` can be Last 7 Days, Last 14 Days, or Last 30 Days.

This shows a breakdown of your message activity, voice time, and reactions over the selected period.

## Privacy

```
/stats privacy
```

Controls whether your activity is tracked and whether you appear on leaderboards. Turning this off going forward doesn't erase history that was already recorded — see [Reset](#reset-your-data) if you want that too.

## Ignore specific channels or roles

```
/stats ignore
```

Exclude specific channels from counting toward your stats — useful if you're active in a channel that shouldn't affect your numbers (like a bot-testing channel).

## Weekly reports

```
/stats reports
```

Enable or disable a personal weekly summary of your activity, delivered automatically.

## Reset your data

```
/stats reset
```

Permanently deletes your personal stats history in this server. This can't be undone.
