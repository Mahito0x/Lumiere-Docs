---
title: Leaderboards
description: See who's most active in your server.
---

```
/stats leaderboard category
```

Three categories to choose from:

- **Top Chatters** — ranked by messages sent
- **Top Voice Users** — ranked by time spent in voice channels
- **Most Engaging** — ranked by reactions given and received

Anyone who's opted out via [`/stats privacy`](/stats-and-leaderboards/your-stats#privacy) won't appear on leaderboards, even if they were active before opting out.
