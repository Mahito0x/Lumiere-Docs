Lumière quietly tracks server activity — messages, voice time, and reactions — and turns it into stats you can view per member, per channel, or server-wide. All of it is grouped under a single `/stats` command with different views.

## What's in this section

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Your Stats](/stats-and-leaderboards/your-stats) | Personal stats, privacy, and weekly reports |
| [Server Analytics](/stats-and-leaderboards/server-analytics) | Channel and server-wide activity, admin tools |
| [Leaderboards](/stats-and-leaderboards/leaderboards) | Top chatters, voice users, and most engaging members |

## Privacy comes first

Any member can opt out of stats tracking at any time — see [Your Stats](/stats-and-leaderboards/your-stats#privacy). Opting out stops new data collection; it doesn't retroactively delete what's already been recorded unless you also reset your data.
