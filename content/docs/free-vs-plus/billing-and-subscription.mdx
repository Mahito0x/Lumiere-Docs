title: Billing & Subscription
description: Managing payment, renewals, and cancellations for Lumière Plus.
---

Billing for Lumière Plus is handled entirely through the [lumierelabs.xyz](https://lumierelabs.xyz) dashboard, not through Discord commands.

## Managing your subscription

From the dashboard, after selecting your server, you can:

- View your current plan and renewal date
- Update your payment method
- View past invoices
- Cancel your subscription

## What happens if you cancel

Your server keeps Plus access until the end of the current billing period. After that, the server automatically returns to the free tier — no data is deleted, but limits and Plus-only features go back to the free defaults (for example, backups beyond the free limit stop being accessible for creation, though existing ones aren't deleted immediately — see [Managing Backups](/server-backups/managing-backups)).

## Questions about billing

For anything not covered here — refunds, payment issues, invoicing for an organization — reach out through the contact details on [lumierelabs.xyz](https://lumierelabs.xyz).
