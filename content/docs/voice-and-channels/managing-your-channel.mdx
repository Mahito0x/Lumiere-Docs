---
title: Managing Your Channel
description: Everything you can do once you own a temporary voice channel.
---

Once you've joined a Join-to-Create channel and gotten your own voice channel, all of these commands are grouped under `/voice`. You need to be the current owner of the channel to use most of them.

## Basics

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/voice rename name` | Rename your channel |
| `/voice limit limit` | Set a user limit (`0` = unlimited) |
| `/voice bitrate bitrate` | Set audio bitrate (8–384 kbps, depending on your server's boost level) |
| `/voice nsfw` | Toggle NSFW status |
| `/voice region region` | Set a specific voice region, or Automatic |
| `/voice status status` | Set a status message shown on your channel |
| `/voice game` | Auto-rename your channel based on what you're currently playing |

## Privacy & access

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/voice lock` | Lock the channel so others can't join without permission |
| `/voice unlock` | Unlock it again |
| `/voice ghost` | Hide the channel from the channel list |
| `/voice unghost` | Make it visible again |
| `/voice permit` | Allow specific users or roles into your locked channel |
| `/voice reject` | Revoke access and kick specific users or roles out |
| `/voice invite user` | Ping a user to invite them into your channel |

## Requesting access

If a channel is locked and you're not on the permit list, you can ask to join:

```
/voice request channel
```

This sends a request to the channel owner rather than letting you in automatically.

## Ownership

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/voice claim` | Claim an abandoned channel (its original owner has left) |
| `/voice transfer user` | Hand ownership over to someone else in the channel |

## If your control panel disappears

```
/voice panel
```

Resends the interactive control panel to your channel, in case it got scrolled away or accidentally dismissed.
