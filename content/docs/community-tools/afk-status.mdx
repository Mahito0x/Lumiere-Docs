---
title: AFK Status
description: Let the server know you're away, and catch up on mentions when you're back.
---

## For members

### Set your AFK status

```
/afk set reason duration
```

- `reason` — why you're away (shown to anyone who mentions you).
- `duration` — optional auto-clear timer, e.g. `30m`, `2h`, `1d`. Leave empty and your AFK status stays until you clear it yourself.

While you're AFK, anyone who mentions you will see your status and reason. Lumière also tracks who mentioned you while you were away, so you can catch up.

### Check your status

```
/afk status
```

Shows your current AFK state and any mentions you've received while away.

### Cancel your AFK status manually

```
/afk cancel
```

Your AFK status also clears automatically the next time you send a message, so you often won't need this — it's there for when you want to come back without posting anything first.

## For admins

Admin commands are grouped under `/afkadmin` and require **Manage Server**.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/afkadmin clear user` | Clear AFK status for a specific member |
| `/afkadmin clearall` | Clear AFK status for every member in the server |
| `/afkadmin list` | See everyone currently AFK |
| `/afkadmin panel` | Manage server-wide AFK behavior and whitelist settings |
