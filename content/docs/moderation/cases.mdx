---
title: Cases
description: Every moderation action, recorded and searchable.
---

Every ban, kick, mute, warn, and strike in Lumière automatically creates a **case**. Cases are your server's permanent moderation record — searchable by user, action type, or status.

## View a case

```
/case info case_id
```

Leave `case_id` empty to see your most recently created case. No special permission required to view a case's basic info, though sensitive details may be limited depending on your role.

## List cases

```
/case list user action state
```

All filters are optional:

- `user` — only show cases involving this member.
- `action` — filter by type: Ban, Kick, Mute, Warn, or Strike.
- `state` — Open or Closed.

Run it with no filters to see every case in your server.

## Delete a case

```
/case delete case_id all
```

Deletes a single case by ID, or set `all` to `True` to wipe **every** case in your server. This is permanent. Requires **Administrator**.

## Why cases matter

Cases are what powers `/history` — a member's full moderation history is just the list of every case attached to them. Keeping cases intact (rather than deleting them) gives your mod team a complete, honest record over time.
