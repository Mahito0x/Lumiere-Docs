---
title: Bot Offline or Not Responding
description: What to check if Lumière isn't responding at all.
---

## 1. Check Lumière's status in your member list

If Lumière shows as offline in your member list, the bot itself is down — this usually resolves on its own shortly. There's nothing to configure on your end for this case.

## 2. If Lumière shows online but doesn't respond

- Run `/ping` first — if that responds, the bot is fine and the issue is likely specific to one command (see [Common Issues](/troubleshooting/common-issues)).
- Check that Lumière has **Send Messages** and **View Channel** permission in the channel you're testing in. A missing channel-level permission override is a common, easy-to-miss cause.
- Try the command in a different channel to confirm whether it's channel-specific or server-wide.

## 3. Slash commands not appearing at all

Slash command registration can occasionally lag behind, especially right after Lumière joins a server or after an update. Give it a few minutes; if commands still aren't showing up after that, try kicking and re-inviting the bot.

## 4. Still nothing?

If none of the above resolves it, it's likely an issue on Lumière's end rather than your server's configuration. Check our [Status Page](https://status.lumierelabs.xyz) for ongoing incident reports.
