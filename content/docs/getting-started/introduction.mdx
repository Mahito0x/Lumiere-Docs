Lumière is a multi-purpose Discord bot. Instead of installing five different bots for moderation, roles, voice channels, and games, you install Lumière once and turn on the pieces you need.

## What Lumière can do

- **Moderation** — bans, kicks, timeouts, warnings, a full case history, automatic message purging, and conduct tracking.
- **Roles & Access** — roles on join, roles that survive a member leaving and rejoining, temporary roles, and bulk role tools.
- **Voice & Channels** — a Join-to-Create system so members get their own voice channel on demand, with full owner controls.
- **Community Tools** — AFK status, birthdays, sticky messages, counters, a counting game, and reaction-based highlight boards.
- **Engagement & Games** — trivia, minesweeper, tic-tac-toe, a memory game, and a large set of social and fun commands.
- **Stats & Leaderboards** — message, voice, and reaction stats per user, per channel, and server-wide.
- **Server Backups** — full server backups you can restore, duplicate, encrypt, or turn into a reusable template.

## Slash commands and prefix commands

Most Lumière commands work as Discord slash commands (type `/` and pick the command). Many also work as classic prefix commands using your server's configured prefix (see [`/prefix`](/getting-started/quick-setup)). Slash commands are recommended — they show you exactly what each option does as you type.
