## Play

```
/trivia play
```

Answer a random trivia question. You can narrow things down with category and difficulty options:

**Categories:** Any Category, General Knowledge, Books, Film, Music, Video Games, Science & Nature, Computers, Mathematics, Sports, Geography, History, Animals

**Difficulty:** Any, Easy, Medium, Hard

## View stats and leaderboards

```
/trivia stats
```

One command with several views, chosen via its option:

- **Leaderboard** — top players in your server
- **Personal Stats** — your own performance
- **Total Points** — ranked by points earned
- **Accuracy (%)** — ranked by correct-answer percentage
- **Trivia Streak** — ranked by longest current streak
- **Fastest Response Time** — ranked by speed

## How scoring works

Trivia tracks your total points, your accuracy across every question you've answered, and your current streak of correct answers in a row — all visible through `/trivia stats`.
